# AI process log

Student-maintained. Record actual interactions in your own words; do not have AI fill this in.

For each session, record:

- Date, approximate time spent, AI tool, and context files used.
- Task/prompt and what the AI produced.
- What you kept, changed, or rejected, and why.
- Who wrote the relevant code and what you can now explain.
- Mistakes, limitations, or unresolved questions.

Across the lab, retain 3-5 representative prompts and briefly explain why you chose them.
